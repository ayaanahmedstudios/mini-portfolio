  • Ayaan Ahmed Brand Identity • 2019+ Brand Identity 2019+

    I started developing this brand in the first few days I began learning how to use Illustrator, to reflect my personal identity and the modern, minimal style I often use in my design work. The brand has since been used on projects, videos, assignments and social media.

    The main logo incorporates multiple concepts and elements, like utilizing the modern Lemon/Milk font, combining the left strokes of the As in my first and last name, and a hidden emoticon within the E and D in my last name.

  • Blue Tiger Inc Studios Brand Identity • 2017+ Brand Identity 2017+

    In 2017, I started writing a book: half fiction, and half autobiography. Its title became my primary online persona, and my outlet for my creative projects. The main character was me, and it made me the Blue Tiger.

    Under the brand and persona of Blue Tiger Inc Studios, I created hundreds of videos since its inception, and gained thousands of subscribers on its main outlet on YouTube. The original book is still ongoing in writing.

  • Ayaan Was Here Brand Identity • 2022 Brand Identity 2022

    Life is a series of events, certain events shine out. I wanted to archive mine, in my own creative way. What resulted was Ayaan Was Here, a digital archive of key accomplishments, dates and moments that show that: I was here.

    Ayaan Was Here primarily exists as a unified designed system on Instagram with ongoing posts from the key dates across my life and more to come in the future.

  • Confounding Tools Poster Design • 2025 Poster Design 2025

    As the first assignment for a second-year Graphic Design class, I was tasked with developing a cohesive, well designed poster WITHOUT the usage of any design-specific software, which spiralled into an in-depth production of variations across multiple platforms and software.

    The final poster came about from the usage of multiple of my own photographs, using the video editing software Final Cut Pro as the main canvas instead of design software, and some additional touch up on some assets with Krita.

  • Flying Colors Poster Design • 2025 Poster Design 2025

    Flying Colors is a research-backed poster for a fictional exhibition about the design of airplane liveries. The poster development underwent about a month of peer research, enhancement and development, and printing.

    The final poster is a thoroughly thought-out poster utilizing some of the many images compiled for the project along with typography and colors tested multiple times for printing until finalized to this version.

  • Designed for Good Print Design • 2025 Print Design 2025

    Designed for Good is a small collection of print design elements based on a research question I posed about the effectiveness of accessible design to individuals with visual impairments, specifically fonts designed for visual accessibility.

    The collection is a group of two postcards and a brochure, purposely designed without completion in mind, as more revolved around the process of finding a research topic to develop design from.

  • Book of Designer Quotes Book Design • 2025 Book Design 2025

    As the final assignment for one of my first-year Typography classes, I was tasked with designing a book using quotes from designers and their descriptions in a comprehensive, well-designed, typographically-sound book layout.

    The final book designed on InDesign, became an array of colors, varying typographic elements, multiple iterations of process and printed for display.

  • Print & Book Design Print Layout • 2024 Print Layout 2024

    As the final assignment for a first-year design process class, I was tasked with designing a book with the narrative choice of my experience with all the assignments within the two design process classes I had taken up till that point. The catch being the book had to be designed physically on printed and hand-drawn paper rather than any design software.

    The final book was made up of collaging, printing hundreds of words, letters and paragraphs, finding images from old assignments, creating a narrative chapter setup describing my frustrations across the assignments and finally compiled, scanned and printed in a final form.
