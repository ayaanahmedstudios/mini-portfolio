  • Ayaan Ahmed 2024 Brand Identity

    I started developing this brand in the first few days I began learning how to use Illustrator, to reflect my personal identity and the modern, minimal style I often use in my design work. The brand has since been used on projects, videos, assignments and social media.

    The main logo incorporates multiple concepts and elements, like utilizing the modern Lemon/Milk font, combining the left strokes of the As in my first and last name, and a hidden emoticon within the E and D in my last name.

  • Blue Tiger Inc Studios 2024 Brand Identity

    In 2017, I started writing a book titled ‘The Blue Tiger’, which then became a sort of pseudo-production company for any creative projects I made.

    I officially set it up as a studio identity in 2021, and the logo for it went through numerous iterations. The final version relies on the ‘Blue Tiger’ and the ‘Inc’ text hidden in plain sight, with the negative space of the ‘T’ forming an ‘I’, and the entire symbol looking like a ‘C’.

  • Ayaan Was Here 2024 Brand Identity

    Life is a series of events, certain events shine out. I wanted to archive mine, in my own creative way. What resulted was Ayaan Was Here, a digital archive of key accomplishments, dates and moments that show that: I was here.

    Ayaan Was Here primarily exists as a unified designed system on Instagram with ongoing posts from the key dates across my life and more to come in the future.

  • Confounding Tools 2022 Poster Design

    As the first assignment for a 2nd year course at OCAD University, I was required to make a tool out of paper that could be used to do something. So I made a confounding tool that could be used to create randomized, but symmetrical shapes with pen and paper, and named it the “SymmetroGraph”.

    The poster details the tool, how it works, what it makes and its dimensions through its brutalist design.

  • Flying Colors 2022 Poster Design

    Flying Colors is a research-based exploration on how the specific design elements of Flags can be altered without losing the integrity, recognisability, and visual presence of the design, along with an exploration into how these specific design constraints force a creative box upon flag makers, and to what extent those specific constraints can be broken, altered, pushed and squeezed.

    This 11x17 poster features 21 different original Flag Designs, with none conforming to the standard 2:3 rectangular form of most national flags in the world today. Ranging from pennants to squares, this poster archives all of them with specific detailings and proportions noted.

  • Designed for Good 2022 Print Design

    Designed for Good is a small campaign made to address an issue, taking the format of three deliverables, the first being a sticker, second being a poster, and the third being a t-shirt. The issue that was taken into consideration was excessive social media use. The visual identity of these designs takes cues from standard warning symbols with harsh contrast and abrasive typography.

  • Cinema VS Streaming 2022 Data Visualization

    As the final assignment for a 2nd year course at OCAD University, I was required to make an infographic visualizing a set of data over time and finding the human connection to it.

    I decided to visualize the global box office vs the revenue of all the streaming services worldwide. Displayed like a timeline, Cinema sits on the left side, representing the past, while Streaming sits on the right, representing the future, with the center showing the present. A secondary smaller graph points out exactly when Streaming officially took over Cinema worldwide.

  • Book of Designer Quotes 2022 Book Design

    As the final assignment for one of my first-year courses, I was tasked with compiling a set of quotes from different designers into a singular book/document. Keeping my minimal aesthetic in mind alongside the stark contrast, this was the final design for the book consisting of 11 spreads, featuring quotes from Saul Bass, Massimo Vignelli, Lance Wyman and more.

  • Design Process Book 2021 Physical Book Design

    As the final assignment for a 2nd year course at OCAD University, I was required to make a physical book chronicling my design process through all the previous assignments throughout the course, this book is 44 pages long and bound by screw posts covered in a black tape spine. To differentiate chapters of the book, I bound it together with yellow stock paper in between chapters so the spine from the side views as a barcode like line system.

  • Crafted from Nature's Blueprints 2020 Speculative Design

    Through the development of a speculative design product meant for the future of Earth, this poster was designed to display what we believe a marketing poster would look like for a device meant to replace extinct bees.

  • Art Journal 2019 Poster Design

    As an ice-breaking assignment for one of my first-year courses, I was tasked with writing and designing something that illustrated my answer to the question: “Why do you make art?”, which was then answered through this poster I designed.

    The poster is primarily separated into 3 sections for a few of my art mediums across my life, those being Drawing and Design, and Filming and Photography. All the illustrations on the poster were designed by me over the years prior, and the photographs were taken by me as well.
